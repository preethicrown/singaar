      The SINGAAR promise

      Fair price. Real quality. Every time.

      This is the standard every artist and salon on SINGAAR will be held to. The prototype shows how it feels. The checks behind it are what we build next.

      Standards we are building
      I

      Real work, checked

      Every artist shows real portfolio work before she is listed. No stock photos, no borrowed looks.

      II

      A price you can trust

      Prices are shown up front and compared with the city, so a lower price never has to feel lesser, and a high one has to earn it.

      III

      Try before the big day

      Trial looks for bridal bookings, so you meet your look before your wedding morning.

      IV

      We stand behind it

      Reviews only from completed bookings, and a real person from SINGAAR steps in if something goes wrong.
