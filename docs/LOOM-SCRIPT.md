# SINGAAR: Loom script (about 4 minutes)

Record on your phone screen or a phone-size browser window. Open **singaar.vercel.app** in a fresh tab first.
Speak slowly. Each block is one scene: **[what you click]** and *what you say*.

---

## 1. The problem (20 s)
**[Home page: logo and search bar]**
*"A bride in Bangalore finds her makeup artist on Instagram, haggles on WhatsApp, and on the wedding morning she just hopes the artist turns up on time. Independent women artists have the opposite problem: they're talented, but nobody can find them, compare them or book them. SINGAAR fixes both. Beauty meets you."*

## 2. Discover (40 s)
**[Scroll to "Your wedding, ritual by ritual". Tap Haldi → Mehendi → Muhurtham]**
*"SINGAAR is built around our rituals: Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, the Muhurtham, the Reception."*
**[Tap "See Muhurtham artists"]**
*"Swipe through real looks."* **[swipe the photo cards, tap a heart]**
**[Drag the Budget slider]** *"Set your budget and the artists update instantly."*
**[Tap "All prices" on a card so it flips]** *"Every price is visible. No haggling."*

## 3. Book (40 s)
**[View profile → Check → fill name and venue → Send booking request]**
*"I check her date and send a request in one step."*
**[Tap "View booking"]** *"My booking shows its progress like Zomato: Requested, Accepted, Advance paid, On the way, Arrived."*
*(The sample artist accepts by herself in about 35 seconds. Or switch to the artist side and accept.)*

## 4. The wow moment: live arrival (40 s)
**[When it says Confirmed: Pay advance (test) → Track arrival]**
*"On the day, I watch her come to me, like a Rapido ride."*
**[Drag "Drag through the morning"]** *"For the muhurtham, the whole morning works backwards from the auspicious time: she leaves, arrives, makeup, draping, ready for photos."*

## 5. The artist side (40 s)
**[Open singaar.vercel.app/partner]**
*"Artists get their own app, SINGAAR Partner, like Uber Driver. They sign in with Google or email and join in five steps: details, services and their own prices, photos of their work, ID verification and UPI. Our team approves every artist before customers can book her, so customers can trust who comes to their home."*
*(If accounts are switched on, show the sign-up steps and /admin approving. If not, show the sign-in screen and say "launching with our first artists".)*

## 6. How it's built (40 s)
*"The frontend is a fast, installable phone web app, hosted on Vercel. The backend is Supabase: Postgres database, sign-in, photo storage and real-time updates, with row-level security so a booking, its chat and its live location are visible only to that customer and that artist. AI is Claude, through OpenRouter. It understands requests like 'muhurtham makeup in Chennai under 20k', writes reply drafts for artists, and a price check blocks any AI message that quotes a price not on the artist's list. Maps use OpenStreetMap. Payments will run on Razorpay."*

## 7. Business model (20 s)
*"We take 10% only when the artist earns, plus a small ₹299 monthly platform fee after three free months. That's far less than the big platforms, so women artists keep most of what they earn."*

## 8. Honest status and next step (20 s)
*"Today the customer side runs on sample artists so you can try the full flow. The partner app, sign-in and approvals are built on a real database. Next, we onboard our first real artists in Bangalore, starting with my mother's network of beauty professionals, switch on Razorpay, then add phone OTP. Thank you."*

---

**Before recording:** clear the browser (or use a private window), so the booking flow starts fresh.
**If something lags:** just keep talking. Never say "it's broken", say "and in the live version…"
