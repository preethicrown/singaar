RAREBeauty, events and services· I001

Finding a trusted bridal makeup artist or saree draper at a fair price

Problem

Women who need bridal makeup, saree draping or similar services for weddings and functions find artists through Instagram or word of mouth. They can't easily compare prices, check quality, or find someone available at short notice. Skilled women who do this part-time have no steady way to get customers.

Who experiences it

Brides, bridesmaids and women attending functions, and the part-time or home-based makeup artists and drapers who serve them.

Challenge

Help customers book a trusted artist at a fair price, and help artists get regular bookings without doing their own marketing.

Build expectation

Run a two-week test in one area. Match at least 5 real customer requests with 10 artists by hand, and compare it with how those customers would normally find someone.

North star metric

Paid bookings per week.

Constraints

Artist ID checks, reviews and a replacement if an artist cancels. Customers and artists going direct after the first booking. Payments by UPI at booking. Customer privacy under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

Why it matters

Weddings and festivals create steady, high-value demand. General beauty platforms don't focus on event work like bridal makeup and saree draping. The open question is whether people will pay a platform fee for trust and convenience.

Directions (examples)

  • matching a request to 2 or 3 artists by date and budget
  • artist profiles with past work, price range and reviews
  • a same-day option for last-minute draping
